    \end{array}
    \right]

\begin{align*}
    \dot{x} &= Ax + Bu \\

A high q/r will give a fast response, but with a high energy drainage. Can expect some overswing as that is a waste of energy. A low q/r will give a slow response where you minimize the amount of energy spent.
